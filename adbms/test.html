3. Choice-pair hypotheses + pick-sides strategy

Say pick sides in the tutor once both sides of a pair are covered: chosen side goes to Red depth (write-memorize-skip.md boxes + timed writing), the other drops to Yellow (skim likely-questions.md MED once, near the exam, in case the slot fills differently).
