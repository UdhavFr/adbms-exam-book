## 5. Unresolved uncertainties (honest list)

1. **No syllabus or past papers available** — priority ranking is inferred from the source's internal structure (its own 20-mark answer bank, "EXAM-READY POINT" markers, diagram practice sheet). If the real exam is 10-mark-heavy, shift time from the 20-mark spines to `comparisons.md` + `definitions.md`.
2. **Question pattern/marking scheme unknown** (20 vs 15 vs 10 marks per long answer) — answer-depth guidance assumes the source's 20-mark framing; the 2/5/10 structures remain valid regardless.
3. **BCNF has no canonical example in the source** — notes explain the rule and the 3NF-vs-BCNF contrast but do not invent a new BCNF example relation (avoided so no fabricated example conflicts with the professor's).
4. **Timestamp-ordering read/write rule variants** (e.g. Thomas' write rule) are summarised at the source's level of detail; the detailed rules are marked **[EXTERNAL]** and are lower priority than the source's own summary.
5. **Page references** depend on Word's pagination of the DOCX (36 pp.); a different Word version/fonts may shift pages by ±1. Section IDs (`u2ch13`) are stable regardless.
6. **Papermorph's own PDF tooling** (`outline.py --level`, PDF page rendering) was not exercised on a PDF because no PDF source exists; the DOCX-adapted pipeline reproduces its outputs (sections + per-section text) instead.
